Origin of the Court

In December 1891, Dr. James Naismith invented basketball at a YMCA in Springfield, Massachusetts, to provide an indoor winter activity. Using a soccer ball and two peach baskets, he created 13 rules, allowing the game to spread quickly and evolve into a global sport. Basketball has transcended its origins as a mere sport to become a profound cultural and social force, significantly influencing society worldwide. As a unifying force, it bridges diverse communities, breaking down cultural and socioeconomic barriers, particularly in urban areas.
